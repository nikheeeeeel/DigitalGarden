Anchor or no anchor?

Ramblings from a rainy afternoon.

What is an anchor?

Is an anchor purpose?

Keeping all of us from straying off from the "right" path. Without an anchor, you are a loose kite. Sorry, the better metaphor would be a ship that cannot stay still.

A still ship has purpose. The anchor holds it in place so the sailor can keep its companion captive. The ship, however, was never made to stay still. It was made to sail the rage and calm of the ocean. It was meant to give its best at taming the ocean.

No.

The ocean was never meant to be tamed. The ship is at its best when the untamed ocean throws everything it has at her and she responds equally, returning the favour. So, isn't the anchor stealing her of her true purpose? Maybe it's just a poor analogy for life. The anchor is a great metaphor for stability, but a very poor one for purpose.

There are different kinds of people. Some feel free on the ground, some in the sky, and some in water. The anchor might be right for the people who feel safe on land, but for the others, stability might sound like restraint.

To them, life was never about stability. For them, purpose doesn't need stability. It needs intoxication. It needs to be set free. Purpose means freedom. It means life itself.

They struggle with synonyms of control, because that means they are moving further away from their true purpose.

The smartest creatures on this planet haven't lived long enough to feel the depth of what it feels like to be human. Being human is a relatively new field of fiction. Something the scientists haven't been able to wrap their heads around.

Santa Claus is also fiction, as they say. But the happiest one can get on Christmas Day is when they believe in that fiction.

To early humans, a lot of the current sciences may sound like fiction. Just so, being human is also in its fiction phase.

Quite selfishly, I am glad it is.

Because for some, it is intoxicating to find meaning in everything. Like the hypocrisy of the anchor.

For those who live in the clouds, each cloud is different. Science would call it moisture, but for some, they are islands of white that know no borders, ever-changing, ever-moving, never still, and always free.

To some, that is the way they want to live.

It might seem silly to the men on the ground. But it is very important to understand that fiction can mean freedom, even to a man of science.
